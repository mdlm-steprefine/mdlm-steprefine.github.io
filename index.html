StepRefine

Rethinking in Spikes: Mitigating Hallucinations in MDLMs with Step-Aware Decoding

Key finding: Localized entropy spikes during denoising are closely associated with hallucinated content in multimodal diffusion language models.

Step-aware triggering: A sliding-window entropy criterion identifies uncertain steps and selectively activates refinement.

Continuous refinement: Probability-weighted token embeddings preserve predictions until consecutive distributions stabilize, before returning to discrete decoding.

Empirical gains: StepRefine improves hallucination, general reasoning, and math/science benchmarks across four multimodal diffusion backbones without training.

Overview

Multimodal diffusion models generate text through iterative denoising. Although average mask entropy generally decreases, a small number of steps show sharp rises. These entropy spikes are closely associated with hallucinated content in the final response.

StepRefine identifies anomalous steps relative to a local temporal window. Instead of immediately committing to a token, it lets candidate semantics interact with the context through probability-weighted embeddings.

Entropy Spike Analysis

Spikes concentrate in early-to-middle denoising, when context is still limited. Perturbing token selection at spike steps changes performance little, while the same perturbation at ordinary steps causes clear degradation.

Method

StepRefine detects entropy spikes, refines uncertain predictions with soft token embeddings, and resumes discrete decoding when the distribution stabilizes.

Detect

Trigger refinement when the entropy change exceeds the sliding-window mean plus its standard deviation.

\[\Delta H_t > \mu_{\mathrm{window}} + \sigma_{\mathrm{window}}\]

Refine

Feed probability-weighted token embeddings back into the model to preserve competing predictions.

\[\widetilde{\mathbf e}_i = \sum_{v\in\mathcal V} p_i(v)\,\mathbf W_{\mathrm{emb}}(v)\]

Stop

Stop when consecutive distributions stabilize, then commit the refined prediction.

\[\frac{1}{|\mathcal M_t|}\sum_{i\in\mathcal M_t} D_{\mathrm{KL}}\!\left(p_i^{(r)}\,\middle\|\,p_i^{(r-1)}\right) < \varepsilon,\qquad \varepsilon = 0.01\]

Ablation Studies

A moderate calibration window

A window of 16 steps balances noisy local estimates and over-smoothed trajectories in the reported experiments.

Adaptive triggering and termination

Entropy-aware triggering outperforms the tested fixed schedules. Monitoring distributional stability also improves over a fixed number of refinement rounds.
